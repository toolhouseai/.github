# Don't build agents. Delegate work.

[Toolhouse](https://toolhouse.ai) turns your work tasks into autonomous AI workers that do real work while you're busy. Delegate tasks in plain language over email, Slack, or Teams, and receive finished deliverables directly back in your inbox.

---

### 🚀 What is Toolhouse?

Toolhouse provides done-for-you AI workers across marketing, sales, support, and operations. Instead of writing code or manually babysitting drafts, you brief your AI worker via email or chat. Toolhouse plans the task, executes it using your connected tools, and delivers completed results.

### ✨ Key Capabilities

- **Email & Messaging Native:** Delegate tasks by sending an email or Slack message—no complex UI or prompt engineering required.
- **5,000+ Integrations:** Seamlessly connects to your existing stack, including Gmail, Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Sheets, Slack, Notion, Zendesk, and more.
- **Model-Agnostic & Deterministic:** Powered by vendor-neutral models grounded on your own data for consistent, reliable outcomes.
- **MCP Server Compatible:** Native support for Model Context Protocol (MCP) to extend Toolhouse with custom tools and systems.
- **Autonomous & Guardrailed:** 24/7 background execution with spending caps, approval checkpoints, and enterprise security controls.

---

### 🛠️ Use Cases

- **Sales & Outreach:** Retrieve carrier/market quotes, enrich inbound leads, update CRMs, and generate proposal decks.
- **Legal & Contracts:** Redline documents, extract key policy terms, and summarize agreement changes.
- **Customer Support:** Resolve tickets automatically from your documentation and escalate recurring issues to engineering.
- **Marketing & Social:** Draft campaign copy, schedule multi-channel social posts at peak times, and generate visual assets.
